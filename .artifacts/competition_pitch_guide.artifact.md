# EventPulse — Competition Pitch & Presentation Guide
**Event:** Idea Thrive Bangladesh 2026 (Youth Marketing Club, IIUC)
**Project:** EventPulse (Real-Time Event Feedback & Audience Analytics Platform)

---

## 1. PPT Slide Structure (Recommended: 4 Slides)

### Slide 1: Title & Vision
- **Project Name:** EventPulse
- **Tagline:** *"Transforming audience feedback into real-time actionable insights."*
- **Team Name & Members:** [Your Team Name & IIUC IDs]

### Slide 2: The Problem
- Traditional event feedback relies on manual paper forms or post-event emails.
- Results are slow, incomplete, biased, and difficult to aggregate.
- Organizers lack live visibility during seminars and workshops to make immediate improvements.

### Slide 3: Our Solution & Core Features
- **Instant QR Access:** Participants scan a QR code at the venue to open the feedback page instantly.
- **Multi-Dimensional Rating:** Overall, Speaker, Content, and Venue satisfaction scores (1–5 stars).
- **Live Organizer Dashboard:** Real-time response tracking and score breakdowns.
- **AI / NLP Comment Analysis:** Automatically categorizes written comments into positive/negative sentiment and key issue tags (e.g., `#SoundQuality`, `#EngagingSpeaker`).

### Slide 4: Impact & Future Scope
- **Impact:** Paperless, instantaneous, and objective feedback for universities, corporate seminars, and conferences.
- **Sustainability:** Scalable subscription model for institutions and event management agencies.

## 3. Anticipated Judge Q&A Prep

* **Q: How does the AI sentiment analysis work?**
  * *A:* It parses comments for sentiment keywords (positive/negative/neutral) and extracts issue tags like sound or pacing, giving organizers structured insights rather than raw unorganized text.
* **Q: Is participant data private?**
  * *A:* Yes, feedback is completely anonymous by default, encouraging honest and constructive responses.
